Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Homelab Backup Tool is a personal application, used only by its owner, to upload backup files from a private home server to that same owner's Google Drive account.

What data is accessed

The tool accesses only the Google Drive files and folders it creates itself, using the Google Drive API, for the sole purpose of storing backup data.

How data is used

Data is used exclusively to store and retrieve the owner's own backup files. It is not analyzed, sold, or shared with any third party.

Data sharing

No data is shared with any other person, service, or organization.

Data retention and deletion

Backup files remain in Google Drive until manually deleted by the owner. Revoking the app's access via Google Account permissions immediately stops all further access.
